LivrCheck Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 October 2026 · Draft

What we collect

Account details (email, name). Profile details you enter (age, gender, height, weight, language). Your health check answers and results, blood test values if you enter them, and your daily logs (water, food, exercise, steps, sleep). Coins and streaks you earn.

Why we use it

To calculate and show your results, track your progress over time, personalise your daily goals and run the coin rewards. We do not use it for advertising and we never sell it.

Where it is stored

On secure servers run by our database provider (Supabase). Every record is protected so that only your account can read it. Data is encrypted in transit.

Who can see it

Only you. Our team does not look at individual records except to fix a problem you report, or where the law requires it. We do not share your data with third parties.

How long we keep it

For as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, all your data is deleted straight away.

Your rights

You can see and edit your profile in the app at any time, and delete your account and all your data yourself (Profile → Settings → Delete my account) — it is erased immediately. You can also ask us to delete your account without the app. For a copy of your data or any other request, email support@livrcheck.example.

Not medical advice

LivrCheck is a screening and wellness tool. It does not diagnose any condition. Always talk to a doctor about your health.

Changes

If this policy changes we will ask you to agree again in the app.
