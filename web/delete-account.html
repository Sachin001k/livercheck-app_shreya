Delete your LivrCheck account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open LivrCheck and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → ⚙️ Settings → Delete my account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm.

Your account and all your data are erased immediately.

Without the app

Email support@livrcheck.example from the email address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my LivrCheck account”. We will delete your account within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

Everything: your account (email, name), profile (age, gender, height, weight, language), health check answers and results, blood test values, daily logs, coins, streaks and survey answers. Nothing is kept.
